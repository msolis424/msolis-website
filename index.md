## About Me


![Profile Picture]({{ "assets/images/profile-pic.jpg" | relative_url }}){: class="profile-image"}

 
My name is Mark Solis and I am a current senior student at Cornell University. My major is in Mechanical Engineering at the Sibley School of Mechanical and Aerospace Engineering. I have a strong interest in **Dynamics & Control Systems** and **Robotics & Mechatronics**. 

I enjoy modeling systems as differential equations using techniques learned from Dynamics and System Dynamics and translating my designs from theory to physical systems by building sensor-actuator circuits and programming microcontrollers.

### Key Technical Focus Areas
* **System Dynamics & Control:** Mathematical modeling, simulation, and transient response analysis.
* **Foundations of Robotics:** Kinematics, trajectory generation, and sensor feedback integration.
* **Mechatronics & Embedded Systems:** Microcontroller programming, hardware-in-the-loop actuation, and circuit integration.

### Field Work
During acedemic breaks I use my free time to work as an intern. At Lasticks Aerospace I gained many valuable skills realted to what it takes to bring engineering designs into production. 
* **General Dimensioning & Tolerancing (GD&T):** Engineering Drafting
* **Coordinate Measuring Machine (CMM):** Programming experience with **PC-DMIS**. 
* **Metrology**: Experience with Mitutoyo, Mahr, Hexagon, and Renishaw equipment. (Micrometers, Height Gauges, Software, etc.) 
* **CAD**: Experience with Siemens NX.
* **CNC Machining & Tooling (Doosan PUMA SMX2600ST):** Exposure to multi-axis mill-turn operations, setup/fixturing procedures, tool selection, and maintaining work coordinate offsets with tool wear.
