## About Me


![Profile Picture]({{ "assets/images/profile-pic.jpg" | relative_url }}){: class="profile-image"}

 
My name is Mark Solis and I am a current senior student at Cornell University. My major is in Mechanical Engineering at the Sibley School of Mechanical and Aerospace Engineering. I have a strong interest in **Dynamics & Control Systems** and **Robotics & Mechatronics**. 

I enjoy modeling systems as differential equations using techniques learned from Dynamics and System Dynamics and translating my designs from theory to physical systems by building sensor-actuator hardware and programming microcontrollers. My highlighted techniques:
* **System Dynamics & Control:** Mathematical modeling, simulation, and transient response analysis.
* **Foundations of Robotics:** Kinematics, trajectory generation, and sensor feedback integration.
* **Mechatronics & Embedded Systems:** Microcontroller programming, hardware-in-the-loop actuation, and circuit integration.
